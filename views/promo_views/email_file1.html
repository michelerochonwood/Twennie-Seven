Proposal Win Strategy AI Companion

Teach your AI to evaluate proposals like an experienced proposal strategist.

Artificial intelligence has become remarkably good at generating proposal content. What many proposal teams have not yet discovered is that AI can be even more valuable as a proposal reviewer.

The new Proposal Win Strategy AI Companion teaches ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Claude, Gemini, Fuel iX and similar systems how to conduct a structured Blue Team review using Twennie’s Ten Steps to a Win Theme methodology.

Instead of asking AI to simply “review this proposal,” you teach it exactly what an experienced proposal strategist looks for before a proposal is submitted.

Use AI to improve the thinking behind the proposal before using it to improve the words.

In this feature, you’ll learn how to:

• Build a dedicated proposal evaluation assistant.
• Train your AI once instead of writing a new prompt for every proposal.
• Provide the pursuit documents needed for a stronger review.
• Evaluate strategy, evidence and differentiation before rewriting.

Why this matters

The strongest proposals do more than read well. They demonstrate a deep understanding of the client, present meaningful evidence, clearly differentiate the firm and tell one consistent competitive story. This Companion helps your AI evaluate those things before the proposal reaches your client.
